# Code of Conduct

FlowTrace adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to every project space: issues,
pull requests, discussions, and any event or channel where someone represents the project.

## In short

* Be respectful and constructive. Critique results and code, not people.
* Welcome newcomers. Questions about CFD basics are on topic.
* No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing others' private information.
* Engineering disagreements are settled with evidence: a test case, a reference, a measurement.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers. Use the contact listed in the
repository profile, or open a minimal issue asking a maintainer to contact you without giving
details. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict
participation, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
